    How to play

    1. Listen to the 25-second excerpts. No titles — just the music.
    2. Drag them (or use ↑ ↓) into the order you think they were composed, earliest at the top.
    3. Reveal to see the real timeline and the story behind each piece.

    Scoring. Every pair of cards you put in the right relative order earns a point — 15 pairs in a set of six. A random guess gets about half.

    Daily is the same puzzle for everyone. Practice gives you unlimited Beethoven and Brahms sets. Build a set lets you choose composers and types of music — and add historical moments to place on the timeline too.

    What counts as “earlier”?

    The year the work was completed — not when it was recorded, published or arranged. No two cards in a set share a year. Single-composer sets take two works from each of the early, middle and late career; mixed sets take one from each period for both composers.
