    How to play

    1. Listen to six 25-second excerpts. No titles — just the music.
    2. Drag them (or use ↑ ↓) into the order you think they were composed, earliest at the top.
    3. Reveal to see the real timeline and the story behind each piece.

    Scoring. Every pair of excerpts you put in the right relative order earns a point — 15 pairs in all. A random guess averages 7.5.

    Daily is the same puzzle for everyone, so you can compare with friends. Practice gives you unlimited sets for Beethoven, Brahms, or a mix of both.

    What counts as “earlier”?

    The year the work was completed — not when it was recorded, published or arranged. Every set has six different years. Single-composer sets take two works from each of the early, middle and late career; mixed sets take one from each period for both composers.
